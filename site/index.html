The problem

Your robots are working. Your capital shouldn’t be stuck in them.

  1. 01

    Banks don’t price robots

    A general lender sees an unfamiliar asset and asks for more collateral or a personal guarantee.

  2. 02

    Vendor leases lock you in

    Financing tied to one maker limits which machines you deploy next.

  3. 03

    Buying outright stalls growth

    Every unit paid in cash is a unit you can’t add next quarter.

Our approach

We lend against the machine, not just your balance sheet.

Each robot is valued on its own — model, condition and how hard it works — so the robot itself carries the loan.

Valuation

We value the robot from how it actually works.

  • Hours and utilization

    A busy robot is a productive asset.

  • Maintenance and faults

    Service history and error logs show condition.

  • Model, age and resale

    What the unit would fetch on the secondary market.
